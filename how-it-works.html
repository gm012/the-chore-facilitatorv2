How it works

Let’s take it one conversation at a time.

From your first enquiry to a possible placement, we work through the practical details with you.

The starting point is your home, your routine and the help you need.

Tell us about your home.

Tell us which kind of help you need and a little about your daily routine.

Work through the practical details.

We ask about hours, duties, languages, reading and writing, driving and school arrangements. Meals, pets, errands, travel and First Aid requirements can be discussed too.

Consider candidates who may suit the role.

We look at experience, availability and working preferences alongside what your household needs. This does not guarantee a match.

Connect and discuss the role.

When a candidate may suit the role, we help both sides discuss the work, expectations and proposed arrangement.

The next step

Agree the arrangement together.

Both sides need to be comfortable with the next step.

If both sides want to proceed, the role and working arrangement can be agreed. An introduction does not guarantee a placement.

Questions you may have

A little more clarity.

What information should I have ready?

Start with the type of help you need, the location, hours and duties. You can discuss the finer details with us later.

Can I apply if I’m looking for work?

Yes. Use the candidate application to tell us about your experience, availability and preferred work.

Open the candidate application
Is payroll part of every placement?

No. If needed, a candidate may be placed on The Chore Facilitator’s payroll. This is optional and must be agreed separately.

Should I send sensitive documents now?

Please do not include identity documents or medical records in the public forms. Supporting documents can be discussed privately later.
