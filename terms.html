Clear information about using this website.

01

Agreement to these terms

By accessing or using this website, you agree to use it responsibly and in accordance with these Terms of Use.

If you do not agree with these terms, you should not use the website to submit personal information or requests.

02

Purpose of the website

This website provides information about The Chore Facilitator, its placement services and the general process available to clients and candidates.

It may also provide online forms through which clients can submit placement information, candidates can apply for consideration and users can make general enquiries.

03

Placement services

Our main role is to help clients and candidates connect for a placement.

Its role may include understanding a client's placement requirement, considering candidate information and facilitating a connection between the parties.

IMPORTANT

Submitting a placement request does not guarantee that a suitable candidate will be available or that a placement will ultimately be made.

04

Candidate applications

Candidates may submit information for consideration for suitable placement opportunities.

Submission of an application does not mean that a candidate has been approved, employed, shortlisted or guaranteed a placement.

Further information, conversations, references, documents or verification may be required before a candidate can progress through a placement process.

05

Client placement information

Clients are responsible for providing information that accurately reflects the role and household requirements relevant to the placement.

This may include duties, hours, working arrangements, language requirements, transport requirements and other relevant practical information.

Material changes to the placement requirement should be communicated where they may affect candidate suitability or the agreed service.

06

Optional payroll arrangements

The Chore Facilitator's primary service is placement.

Where required and separately agreed, selected candidates may also be placed on The Chore Facilitator's payroll as part of the agreed service.

Any specific payroll arrangement, responsibilities, charges or employment conditions should be governed by the separate agreement applicable to that placement.

07

Users should provide information that is accurate and not intentionally misleading.

The Chore Facilitator may rely on information supplied by clients, candidates and other parties when progressing an enquiry or placement.

Users should notify The Chore Facilitator where important information previously supplied becomes materially inaccurate or changes.
