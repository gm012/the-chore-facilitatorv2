Privacy policy

Clear information about using this website.

01

Overview

The Chore Facilitator provides people placement services connecting clients with candidates for roles including nannies, domestics, night nurses and housekeepers.

Personal information may be needed in order to respond to enquiries, understand placement requirements, review candidate applications and facilitate suitable placement processes.

Information should only be collected where it is reasonably required for the relevant service or conversation.

02

Information we may collect

Client information

This may include a client's name, contact details, general location, household information and information about the placement being requested.

Placement information may include the type of role required, duties, working hours, language requirements, live-in or live-out arrangements, driving requirements, household routine and other practical preferences.

Candidate information

Candidate information may include a person's name, contact information, area, work experience, availability, preferred working arrangement, languages, driving information, First Aid information and reference availability.

General enquiries

When someone contacts The Chore Facilitator through the website, information such as their name, email address, contact number, enquiry type, subject and message may be collected.

03

How information may be used

Personal information may be used to understand and respond to an enquiry, review an application, prepare or clarify a placement brief, communicate with a client or candidate and support the placement process.

Information may also be used for reasonable administrative, record-keeping, security and operational purposes connected with the service being requested.

Personal information should not be used for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate reason or permission.

04

Sensitive information

Some placement conversations may involve information that is more sensitive than ordinary contact information.

The public website is not intended to collect detailed medical information, identity documents or other highly sensitive personal records through ordinary text fields.

IMPORTANT

Where sensitive information is genuinely required for a placement, it should be discussed or provided through an appropriate secure process rather than entered unnecessarily into a public form.

05

Candidate documents

Supporting documents may be required during later stages of a candidate application or verification process.

These may include documents such as a CV, identification information, references, certificates or other records relevant to the placement.

Where such documents are required, candidates should be provided with an appropriate secure method for supplying them. Sensitive documents should not be published through publicly accessible URLs.

06

Information sharing

Information may be shared where this is reasonably necessary to progress a placement or provide the service requested.

For example, relevant candidate information may need to be considered in relation to a client's placement requirement.

The amount of information shared should be appropriate to the purpose and stage of the placement process.

Information may also need to be provided to service providers or authorities where reasonably required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

07

Information security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Website and backend systems should be designed so that private information, credentials and sensitive documents are not intentionally exposed through public-facing website code or publicly accessible storage.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

08

Information retention

Personal information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate operational requirements or applicable legal obligations.

The exact retention periods used by The Chore Facilitator should be confirmed as part of the final operational and privacy process.

09

Your information and choices

A person may contact The Chore Facilitator regarding personal information supplied through the website or placement process.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, this may include requests relating to access, correction, updating or deletion of personal information.

Contact details for formal privacy requests should be added to this policy before the website is launched publicly.

10

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, placement process, technology or information-handling practices change.

The version published on this website should represent the current policy.

Last updated: November 2026

Website forms and external fonts

Enquiries and applications submitted through the live website are saved in a private placement-management database for authorised staff to review. Administrative email notifications contain a record reference rather than the full questionnaire. Supporting documents requested later are stored privately and can be downloaded only by authorised Super Admin staff. A temporary session cookie and short-lived request counters help protect forms against misuse. Do not include identity documents or medical records in these forms.

Fonts are hosted locally with this website; loading them does not contact Google Fonts. No Google Analytics or advertising tracker is installed on this website.

For privacy enquiries, email admin@thechorefacilitator.co.za.

Cookie choices

Cookie settings in the footer lets you review and change your choice. When you save a choice, we store it in your browser for up to 180 days. No optional analytics or marketing service is currently installed. If such a service is introduced, it will remain off until you allow it. Rejecting or withdrawing optional consent does not prevent you from using the website. Supported browser privacy signals keep optional tracking off.

The client-review version does not send form submissions or enquiry emails.
