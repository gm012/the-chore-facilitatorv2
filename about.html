About The Chore Facilitator

People first.

Why we’re here

Getting to know you comes first.

You bring your household, your routines and your expectations. A candidate brings their experience, availability and preferences.

Our role is to understand both sides and help you work through what a placement could look like. We are a placement agency for Nannies, Domestic support, Night Nurses and Housekeepers.

Our approach

A little understanding
goes a long way.

We start with the everyday details, because they matter to the people doing the work and the people welcoming them into their homes.

We listen.

Tell us where you could use an extra pair of hands and what matters in your home.

We clarify.

We talk through the role, hours and practical requirements so expectations are understood.

We connect.

We consider candidates whose experience and availability may suit the role.
